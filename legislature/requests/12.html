What was the fiscal impact of LB1067?

2026-07-25 01:33:49 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

The fiscal impact of LB1067 primarily involves an increase in state revenue through a temporary adjustment to the documentary stamp tax and the dedication of those funds to workforce housing initiatives. [1, 2]

According to the bill as approved by the Governor:

  • Documentary Stamp Tax Rate Increase: The tax rate upon the transfer of real estate was increased from $2.32 to $3.32 for every $1,000 of value for transfers occurring before January 1, 2032. [2, 3] On and after January 1, 2032, the rate is scheduled to return to $2.32. [3]
  • Revenue Allocation: The additional $1.00 in tax revenue generated by this temporary increase is split equally between two funds:
  • $0.50 to the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund. [2, 4]
  • $0.50 to the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund. [2, 4]
  • Affordable Housing Trust Fund Protections: The bill eliminates authorized transfers from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund to various other state funds (such as the General Fund or Behavioral Health Services Fund). [5, 6] Furthermore, starting July 1, 2027, the law prohibits any money from being transferred out of the Affordable Housing Trust Fund, effectively ring-fencing it for housing purposes. [3, 5]
  • Distribution of Total Tax: Out of each $3.32 collected (prior to 2032), $0.50 is retained by the county register of deeds for the county general fund, while the remaining $2.82 is remitted to the State Treasurer to be distributed among various trust funds, including those for affordable housing, homeless shelter assistance, and behavioral health services. [4]

While the initial version of the bill proposed a larger $1.50 increase, the adopted committee amendment (AM2213) set the increase at $1.00 and established the 2032 sunset date. [2, 6] The overall intent was to ensure that documentary stamp tax revenues are accessible and expended for the advancement of affordable and workforce housing. [7, 8]
