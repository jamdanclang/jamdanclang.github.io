I need to know the key points of the Renewable Chemicals tax credit.

2026-08-30 20:58:55 · Sessions 109B,109A,108B,108A,107B,107A,106B,106A,105B,105A · deep

Key points

Nebraska adopted the Renewable Chemical Production Tax Credit Act in LB1107. The final-reading bill text provides:

  • Eligible products: Renewable chemicals are building-block chemicals with significant biobased content used in products such as polymers, plastics, food additives, solvents, intermediate chemicals, supplements, vitamins, nutraceuticals, and pharmaceuticals. Chemicals sold or used as fuel are excluded. [106B-1]
  • Business eligibility: A business must produce at least 1 million pounds of renewable chemicals in Nebraska during the credit year, be physically located in Nebraska, have organized, expanded, or located in the state on or after the section's operative date, and comply with its other state incentive agreements. An agreement may certify the business for up to four years. [106B-2]
  • Credit amount: The refundable income-tax credit equals 7.5 cents per pound produced in Nebraska above the business's pre-eligibility production threshold, with a maximum of $1.5 million per business per year. [106B-4] [106B-5]
  • Program limits: The director could approve up to $3 million total for calendar years 2022 and 2023 and up to $6 million per year for 2024 and later years. Applications approved after an annual cap is reached go onto a waitlist in the order received. [106B-3]
  • Start date: No credit is available for renewable chemicals produced before calendar year 2022. [106B-5]

These points reflect the official final-reading language of LB1107; the citations below link directly to the relevant pages of that bill PDF.
