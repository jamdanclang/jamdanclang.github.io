What is happening with legalizing marijuana?

2026-08-07 01:03:54 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

Nebraska is currently implementing a comprehensive regulatory framework for medical cannabis following the passage of voter initiatives in 2024, while also beginning to study the potential for recreational legalization.

Implementation of Medical Cannabis

Following the passage of Initiative Laws 437 and 438 in 2024, the Legislature has introduced several bills to codify and refine the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Patient Protection Act and the Nebraska Medical Cannabis Regulation Act [1-4].

  • Governor's Approval of LB1235: A major development is the passage of LB1235, which was approved by the Governor on April 7, 2026 [5]. This law transitions medical cannabis from a registration model to a licensure-based system and gives the state exclusive authority over cultivation, manufacturing, testing, and dispensing [6, 7].
  • Nebraska Medical Cannabis Commission: This newly created commission is tasked with oversight of the entire industry [3, 8]. Initially, the three members of the Nebraska Liquor Control Commission serve as ex officio members, with the Governor authorized to appoint two additional members [8, 9]. LB934 proposes transitioning this commission to a nonpartisan elected board starting with the 2028 election [10, 11].
  • Patient and Caregiver Registry: The state is establishing a confidential registry for qualified patients and registered caregivers [6]. To qualify, an individual must have a qualifying medical condition (such as cancer, epilepsy, or ALS) and a written recommendation from an authorized health care practitioner [6, 12, 13].
  • Protections for Practitioners: LB933 was introduced to provide legal immunity for health care practitioners, protecting them from arrest, prosecution, or professional disciplinary action solely for recommending medical cannabis to a patient [14-16].

Recreational Cannabis and Interim Studies

While the current laws focus on medical use, there is active legislative interest in broader legalization:

  • LR456: This resolution proposes an interim study to examine the potential implementation of recreational cannabis in Nebraska [17]. The study is intended to analyze the economic impact, required regulatory standards, and reasons for restricted access [17].

Criminal Justice Reform

LB705 contains provisions for addressing past cannabis-related legal issues through two new acts:

  • Nebraska Medical Cannabis Justice Act: This would allow incarcerated individuals to petition for resentencing for eligible cannabis offenses committed before the act's effective date [18, 19].
  • Cannabis Conviction Clean Slate Act: This proposes automatic record relief (sealing of records) for eligible cannabis offenses, beginning in July 2026 [19, 20].

Consumable Hemp Regulation

The Legislature is also clarifying the distinction between marijuana and hemp. LB316 (the Nebraska Consumable Hemp Control Act) seeks to regulate and tax "consumable hemp products" (like CBD) that contain less than 0.3% THC, distinguishing them from medical or recreational marijuana [21-24]. Beginning in 2026, a 10% excise tax is proposed for the retail sale of these products [25, 26].
