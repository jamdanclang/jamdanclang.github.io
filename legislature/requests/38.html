When was ImagiNE Nebraska first enacted?

2026-08-07 01:29:43 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

Based on the provided sources, the exact date of the ImagiNE Nebraska Act's original enactment is not explicitly stated; however, the records indicate that the program was operational and required formal annual reporting to the Legislature beginning in 2021 [1-3].

Key details regarding its implementation and status in the sources include:

  • Reporting Requirements: The Director of Economic Development and the Tax Commissioner were first required to jointly submit an annual report on the Act’s progress, including agreements signed and jobs created, starting in 2021 [1-3].
  • Incentive Structure: The Act was designed to attract business activities that might otherwise locate outside of Nebraska by providing incentives for job creation and capital investment [4, 5].
  • Recent Amendments: More recent legislative actions have modified the Act, such as provisions in 2025 and 2026 that changed credit percentages, introduced requirements for "benefit corporations," and adjusted reporting for workforce training and infrastructure development [3, 6-8].
  • Future Scope: The sources also note that no new applications for incentives under the current structure of the Act may be filed after December 31, 2030 [9, 10].

Information from outside these sources indicates that the ImagiNE Nebraska Act was originally passed by the Nebraska Legislature as part of LB 1107 in 2020, effectively replacing the Nebraska Advantage Act as the state's primary economic incentive program.
