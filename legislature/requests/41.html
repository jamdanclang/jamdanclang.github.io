Lynn Walz's Main Achievements in the Legislature

108th Legislature, 2nd Session

Senator Lynn Walz achieved several key legislative outcomes:

  • Successfully introduced a resolution designating June 27, 2024, as Post-Traumatic Stress Injury Awareness Day and June 2024 as Post-Traumatic Stress Injury Awareness Month in Nebraska [108B-1].
  • Introduced a resolution officially recognizing April 2024 as Occupational Therapy Month in Nebraska [108B-2].
  • Introduced a resolution congratulating Vivian Sanchez on receiving the 2024 Women MAKE Award [108B-3].
107th Legislature, 2nd Session

During the 107th Legislature, Second Session (2022), Senator Lynn Walz achieved several key legislative outcomes:

  • As Chairperson of the Education Committee, she introduced LB1218 (adopting the Teach in Nebraska Today Act) and co-sponsored LB902 (the Nebraska Career Scholarship Act) [1, 2].
  • Introduced LR335, establishing a state goal of 70% postsecondary credential attainment by 2030 [5, 6].
107th Legislature, 1st Session

During the 107th Legislature, First Session (2021), Senator Lynn Walz secured several key legislative achievements in healthcare and education:

  • Introduced LB100 (prohibiting "multiple procedure payment reduction" billing practices under Medicaid for physical, occupational, and speech-language therapies) [1, 2].
  • Delays the integration of long-term care services and supports into the Medicaid managed care program until July 1, 2023 [3, 4].
106th Legislature, 2nd Session

In the 106th Legislature, Second Session (2020), Senator Lynn Walz introduced several key legislative measures:

  • Successfully enacted LB956 to regulate managed care organization provider agreements and mandate clear notices for modified medical assistance [1, 2].
  • Co-introduced and approved LB1188 to establish a superintendent of institutional schools and mandate gender separation at youth rehabilitation centers [3, 4].
106th Legislature, 1st Session

During the 106th Legislature, First Session (2019), Senator Lynn Walz achieved several key legislative outcomes:

  • Introduced LB600, restructuring the Nebraska Children’s Commission and expanding its authority [1-3].
  • Co-sponsored LR393 to honor teachers and initiated critical interim studies evaluating poverty metrics (LR180) [105B-18].
105th Legislature, 2nd Session

Senator Lynn Walz introduced LB998, establishing the Collaborative School Behavioral and Mental Health Program to place social workers in educational service units [1, 2]. She proposed amendments (AM2665, AM2713) to place the program's administration under the State Department of Education [3, 4].

105th Legislature, 1st Session

During the 105th Legislature, First Session (2017), Senator Lynn Walz introduced LB444, which was approved by the Governor on April 27, 2017 [105A-1]. This bill prevents political subdivisions from canceling health insurance for injured first responders who fall below minimum working hours due to on-duty assault injuries [2, 3].

Additional Cited Findings
