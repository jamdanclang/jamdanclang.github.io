What were the most recent income tax changes?

2026-08-22 14:48:41 · Sessions 109B,109A,108B,108A,107B,107A,106B,106A,105B,105A · deep

Recent Income Tax Changes in Nebraska

The most recent income tax changes enacted in Nebraska are as follows:

  • LB998 (Approved April 14, 2026): Broadens state tax deductions for National Guard members to exclude 100% of Title 10 and Title 32 service income starting in tax year 2027 [1, 2].
  • LB803: Adopts the First-Time Home Buyer Savings Account Act, allowing individuals to deduct contributions up to \$5,000 (single) or \$10,000 (joint) starting January 1, 2027 [3, 4].
  • LB901 (Approved April 7, 2026): Enacts the Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act, establishing \$6 million in annual refundable, transferable income tax credits starting January 1, 2027 [5-7].
  • LB1165: Authorizes a 5% wage retention credit under the Grow the Good Life Act for employers undergoing ownership changes [8-10].

Failed Proposals

Some notable failed proposals include:

  • LB1206: Sought a 100% tax exclusion for educators [11, 12].
  • LB974: Proposed an excise tax on incomes exceeding \$1 million [13, 14].
  • LB872: Proposed eliminating capital gains deductions [15, 16].

Previous Income Tax Changes

Nebraska has enacted several significant income tax changes in previous legislative sessions. Some notable examples include:

  • LB647 (Approved June 4, 2025): Adopted the Adoption Tax Credit Act, offering a refundable credit equal to 10% of the federal credit [1-3].
  • LB182 (Approved February 25, 2025): Expanded the Affordable Housing and Child Care Tax Credits, allowing pass-through entities to allocate housing credits directly to partners [9-11].

Additional Cited Findings

The provided legislative sources do not contain information regarding recent income tax changes enacted during Nebraska's 108th Legislature, First Session. However, they do provide insight into other tax-related proposals and studies conducted during this session.

  • LR314: Proposed interim study to assess the loss of county funding and evaluate potential alternative revenue sources to compensate for a projected five-year phaseout of the Nebraska inheritance tax by 2029 [1-3].
  • LR367: Proposed study to analyze the valuation process across different real property classes and identify mechanisms to flatten rapid valuation increases [4-7].

Uncertainty

Due to the limited information available, it is unclear whether any actual income tax changes were enacted during Nebraska's 108th Legislature, First Session.

Comparison of Tax Credits

The Nebraska Property Tax Incentive Act established a refundable income tax credit for school district property taxes paid, starting on or after January 1, 2020 [1-3]. The credit is available to individuals who pay school district property taxes and meet certain eligibility requirements.

Additionally, LB182 (approved February 25, 2025) expanded the Affordable Housing and Child Care Tax Credits, allowing pass-through entities to allocate housing credits directly to partners [9-11].
