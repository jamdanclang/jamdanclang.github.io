What was the fiscal impact of LB1067?

2026-07-25 01:54:37 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

The fiscal impact of LB1067 primarily involves a temporary increase in the documentary stamp tax to fund workforce housing and the implementation of protections for the Affordable Housing Trust Fund [1-3].

According to the sources, the key fiscal components of the bill are:

  • Documentary Stamp Tax Increase: The bill raises the tax imposed on the transfer of real estate from $2.32 to $3.32 for every $1,000 of value (or fraction thereof) [2, 4]. This $1.00 increase is temporary and is scheduled to revert to the original rate of $2.32 on January 1, 2032 [2].
  • Revenue Allocation: The additional $1.00 in tax revenue collected during this period is split evenly between two specific initiatives:
  • $0.50 is credited to the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Fund [4, 5].
  • $0.50 is credited to the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Fund [4, 5].
  • Fund Transfer Restrictions: LB1067 restricts the Legislature's ability to divert money from the Affordable Housing Trust Fund [1, 2]. While transfers to other funds (such as the General Fund, Behavioral Health Services Fund, or Site and Building Development Fund) are permitted until July 1, 2027, the bill stipulates that no money shall be transferred out of the fund beginning on and after that date [1, 2].
  • County Impact: The portion of the tax retained by the register of deeds for the county general fund remains unchanged at $0.50 for each transaction [5].

While the sources outline these specific rate and allocation changes, they do not provide a specific total annual dollar projection for the revenue expected to be generated by the tax increase [3, 4]. The bill was approved by the Governor on April 14, 2026 [2].
