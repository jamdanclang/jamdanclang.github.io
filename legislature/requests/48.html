What bills were requested by the Governor?

2026-08-23 22:21:25 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

During the 109th Legislature, Second Session (109B), the Governor requested the introduction of several key legislative bills covering state budgeting, early education reform, economic development, state security, and revenue adjustments.

The following bills were explicitly introduced at the request of the Governor during the 109B session:

Budget and Appropriations Bills

  • LB 1071: Introduced by Speaker Arch at the request of the Governor [1-3]. This bill represents the Governor's 2026 mid-biennium budget adjustment recommendations for the 2025–2027 biennium [109B-4]. It makes adjustments to appropriations and reappropriations for state agency operations, state aid, and capital construction programs [1, 2, 4].
  • LB 1072: Introduced by Speaker Arch at the request of the Governor [5-7]. As a companion to the budget adjustment bill, this measure authorizes and alters fund transfers (including transfers from the Cash Reserve Fund), creates specific cash funds, and updates statutory programs to implement the Governor's proposed reductions in spending [5-8].

Education and School Discipline Bills

  • LB 1050: Introduced by Senator Murman at the request of the Governor [109B-9]. This bill amends the Nebraska Reading Improvement Act with the goal of improving early literacy [9, 10]. It outlines procedures to identify third-grade students with reading deficiencies, limits their advancement to grade four, and mandates intensive reading intervention courses for retained students [9, 10].
  • LB 1053: Introduced by the Education Committee at the request of the Governor [109B-11]. This bill amends school discipline statutes to eliminate the prohibition on suspending prekindergarten through second-grade students when alternative disciplinary measures prove ineffective [11, 12].

Revenue, Taxation, and Economic Development Bills

  • LB 1109: Introduced by Senator von Gillern at the request of the Governor [109B-13]. This revenue omnibus bill repeals, sunsets, or eliminates various sales and use tax exemptions (including those for data centers, biochips, and community-based energy development) [13-15]. It also transitions the research tax credit under the Nebraska Advantage Act from a refundable to a non-refundable credit and sunsets renewable energy tax credits [14, 15].
  • LB 1165: Introduced by Senator von Gillern at the request of the Governor [16, 17]. This bill establishes the Grow the Good Life Act, which provides capital improvement grants and other incentives to encourage large in-state employers to retain their headquarters and workforce in Nebraska during mergers or changes in corporate control [16-18].
  • LB 1244: Introduced by Senator Murman at the request of the Governor [109B-19]. This bill adjusts revenue policies by eliminating certain sales and use tax exemptions and imposing sales and use taxes on certain services [19, 20].

Government Administration and Efficiency Bills

  • LB 1048: Introduced by Senator Arch at the request of the Governor [21-23]. Aimed at streamlining state administration, this bill eliminates or consolidates numerous reporting requirements across various state agencies [21, 22, 24]. It also changes reporting procedures for the state executive budget, eliminates reports regarding labor negotiations, and abolishes the Suggestion Award Board and the state employee suggestion system [21, 22, 24, 25].
  • LB 1159: Introduced by Senator Lonowski at the request of the Governor [26, 27]. This bill modifies the statutes of the Nebraska Hall of Fame, requiring the official naming of a person to the Hall of Fame [26, 27].

Security and Judicial Operations Bills

  • LB 1096: Introduced by Senator Bostar at the request of the Governor [28, 29]. This bill adopts the Preventing Lethal Agricultural and National Threats Act (and the Critical Infrastructure Protection Act) to protect state assets [28-30]. It establishes criminal penalties for importing high-risk agricultural pathogens, restricts foreign adversary access to critical infrastructure, and permits withholding records associated with critical water infrastructure [29, 30].
  • LB 1228: Introduced by Senator Holdcroft at the request of the Governor [109B-31]. This bill establishes a state docket fee and creates the Supreme Court Operations Cash Fund and the JUSTICE Software Cash Fund to support judicial operations and the state's case management system [31, 32].

Natural Resources and Energy Bills

  • LB 1261: Introduced by Senator DeKay at the request of the Governor [109B-33]. This bill relates to electrical energy and prohibits the use of eminent domain to acquire certain privately owned electric generation facilities [109B-33].
  • LB 1262: Introduced by Senator Andersen at the request of the Governor [109B-34]. This bill adopts the Foreign Adversaries Out of Postsecondary Education Act and outright repeals statutory provisions relating to certain postsecondary education funding reports [109B-34].

*(Note: While LB 261 was also introduced at the request of the Governor [35, 36], it was part of the 109th Legislature, First Session (109A) in 2025 [109B-35].)*
