Elliot Bostar’s Major Accomplishments

During the Nebraska Legislature's 109th Legislature, 2nd Session:

  • Senator Eliot Bostar achieved significant legislative success with LB986, which was approved by the Governor on April 14, 2026, allowing candidates to use campaign funds for security services [109B-1].
  • Several of his proposals were incorporated into other successful legislative packages:
  • LB1064 (Large Load Customer Regulation Act) was integrated into AM2422 to regulate high-capacity electrical interconnections.
  • LB1131, establishing the Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act, was integrated into AM2406 [109B-4].
  • LB1020 (restricting mobile tracking and swatting) was amended into LB935 [109B-5].
  • LB1123 (public safety protections) and LB859 (county conflict counsel) were merged into LB965 [109B-6].

During the 108th Legislature, 2nd Session:

  • Senator Elliot Bostar introduced resolutions to propose interim studies on:
  • The role of health insurance for biomarker testing and barriers patients face in accessing it ([108B-1], [108B-2]).
  • The impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on Nebraska's public and private sectors, specifically highlighting the technology and insurance sectors ([3], [4]).

During the 107th Legislature, 2nd Session:

  • Senator Elliot Bostar achieved several legislative milestones:
  • LB1273 (approved April 18, 2022), providing an income tax deduction to retired law enforcement officers for health insurance premiums.
  • LB754 (approved March 3, 2022), extending a pilot program for commercial air filters in classrooms.
  • He co-introduced LB1241, enacting the Law Enforcement Attraction and Retention Act.

During the 107th Legislature, 1st Session:

  • Senator Elliot Bostar introduced several major legislative proposals:
  • LB 77 (Ensuring Transparency in Prior Authorization Act), requiring physician-led reviews and prohibiting AI-only denials.
  • LB 109, restricting pharmacy benefit manager networks.
  • LB 252, ensuring Medicaid coverage for non-opioid drugs.
  • LB 253, covering biomarker testing.
