What were the bills passed in the 2022 legislative sessions that affected the Nebraska Department of Economic Development?

2026-08-05 19:37:38 · Sessions 107B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 107th Legislature, 2nd Session

Several bills passed during the 2022 Nebraska legislative session significantly impacted the Nebraska Department of Economic Development (DED), primarily through the creation of new programs, the creation of a new division, and the allocation of substantial federal and state funding.

Major New Acts and Structural Changes

  • Economic Recovery Act (LB 1024): This act created the Economic Recovery and Incentives Division within the DED [1, 2]. It task the department with administering a coordinated plan for economic recovery, specifically targeting areas and populations disproportionately impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic, including those in qualified census tracts [3-5].
  • Jobs and Economic Development Initiative (JEDI) Act (LB 1023): This act was established to attract jobs and investment through water and recreational projects, with the DED playing a role in administering associated funds [6-8].
  • Nebraska Higher Blend Tax Credit Act (LB 1261): This act, administered in coordination with the Department of Revenue, created refundable tax credits for retail dealers of high-blend ethanol fuel [9, 10]. It also extended the sunset date and increased funding thresholds for the Nebraska Advantage Rural Development Act [11, 12].

Significant Funding and ARPA Allocations (LB 1014)

As the primary vehicle for federal American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds, LB 1014 allocated hundreds of millions of dollars to DED-administered programs:

  • Workforce Housing: $50 million for the Rural Workforce Housing Investment Act and $25 million for the Middle Income Workforce Housing Investment Act [13, 14].
  • Site and Building Development: $60 million for projects that address public health measures or economic hardship caused by the pandemic [15, 16].
  • Shovel-Ready Capital Recovery: $100 million for grants to capital projects eligible under the Shovel-Ready Capital Recovery and Investment Act [17-19].
  • International Competitions: $1.5 million for major sponsorship grants for international competitions hosted in Nebraska [20].

Modifications to Existing Programs

  • Business Innovation Act (LB 1012, LB 598): Amendments included the addition of innovation hubs (iHubs) to the act’s scope, redefined economic redevelopment areas, and increased the annual total and award limits for microloan programs [21-24]. LB 598 further adjusted award limitations for prototype creation and value-added agriculture programs [25-28].
  • Site and Building Development Fund (LB 1012, LB 977): Provisions were changed to allow financial assistance for a public-private partnership facility in conjunction with the United States Strategic Command [24, 29-32]. LB 977 also authorized grants for cities of the second class partnering with public power utilities to expand electrical system capacity [33, 34].
  • Internship Programs (LB 1012): The 2022 legislation expanded eligibility, increased grant award amounts for internships, and authorized the DED to contract with a nonprofit entity to carry out program provisions [24, 35].
  • Municipal Inland Port Authority Act (LB 998): The DED was given new duties to review and prioritize inland port authority proposals and provide financial assistance through a specific subaccount of the Site and Building Development Fund [36, 37].
  • ImagiNE Nebraska Act (LB 1150): The session resulted in adjustments to the calculation of equivalent employees, specifically accounting for employees who work from their personal Nebraska residence [38].

Specialized Initiatives

  • Nebraska Hydrogen Hub Industry Work Group (LB 1099): The DED was directed to create this work group to develop a competitive proposal for Nebraska to be selected as a regional clean hydrogen hub under federal law [39].
  • Advanced Nuclear Reactor Study (LB 1100): This bill appropriated $1 million for the DED to provide a grant to a political subdivision for a feasibility study regarding the siting of advanced nuclear reactors in the state [40, 41].
