Nebraska BIA Impact Explorer

Jamdan Clang

When a funding pause for the Nebraska Business Innovation Act (BIA) programs led to an uproar from the Nebraska business community, it became a goal of mine to put BIA's biennial impact analyses to work.

The Talent and Innovation Initiative was passed by the Nebraska Legislature and signed into law in 2011. The Act included the BIA, which is designed to promote successful entrepreneurial firms by providing access to capital in early stages of product development. The BIA provides such support through five primary programs:

  1. the Pre-Seed Prototype grant program;
  2. matching state support for the federal Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) and Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) grants;
  3. the Academic Research and Development grant program;
  4. the Seed/Commercialization program; and the
  5. Microenterprise Loan and Technical Assistance Program.

The studies by the University of Nebraska-Lincoln Bureau of Business Research provide an economic impact assessment of Nebraska businesses that have been supported by the first four BIA programs (the Microenterprise Loan and Technical Assistance Program is excluded from analysis).

Learn more about BIA programs from webpages linked on the Nebraska Department of Economic Development website.

This page gathers data from reports completed in 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, and 2024.
