Key Points of the Renewable Chemicals Tax Credit

The Renewable Chemical Production Tax Credit Act, introduced in the 106th Legislature, provides a refundable tax credit for businesses producing renewable chemicals in Nebraska.

  • The credit rate is 7.5 cents per pound of eligible chemicals produced above a pre-eligibility threshold, capped at \$1.5 million per business annually [4-8].
  • To qualify, a business must be physically located in Nebraska, have organized or expanded on or after the operative date, and produce at least 1 million pounds of renewable chemicals in the state annually [6, 7].
  • The credit is available for eligible chemicals including biobased polymers, plastics, food additives, and pharmaceuticals, but excludes fuel [13-22].

Program Details

  • Certified businesses enter into agreements for up to four years [106A-7].
  • Credits were authorized to begin in calendar year 2022 (LB1107) or 2021 (LB605/LB720) [8, 11].
  • Program-wide limits are set at \$3 million total for calendar years 2022 and 2023, and \$6 million annually for 2024 and beyond, with excess applications placed on a waitlist [8-11, 27].

Uncertainty

The program's funding caps and eligibility requirements may be subject to change or revision.

Additional Cited Findings

  • The Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967 imposes liability on businesses producing certain chemicals [1, 2].
  • Section 77-27,235 allows a tax credit for electricity generated by facilities utilizing eligible renewable resources, including wind, solar, biomass, and methane [1, 2].
  • Certified "high-technology fields" eligible for investment credits specifically include both chemistry and renewable energy [3, 4].
