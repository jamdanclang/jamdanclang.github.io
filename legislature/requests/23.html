What are Elliot Bostar’s legislative priorities?

2026-07-29 01:34:01 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

Based on the sources, Senator Eliot Bostar’s legislative priorities for the 109th Legislature focus heavily on public safety, national security, artificial intelligence regulation, healthcare accessibility, and energy infrastructure.

Public Safety and Law Enforcement

A significant portion of Senator Bostar’s legislation is dedicated to supporting law enforcement and enhancing public safety measures:

  • Law Enforcement Protections: He introduced LB1123 to strengthen due process protections for law enforcement and correctional officers regarding Brady-Giglio disclosures, establishing uniform notice and review requirements [1, 2].
  • Retirement and Benefits: He prioritized the Nebraska State Patrol Retirement Act through LB1103, which proposes raising the mandatory retirement age from 60 to 65 and expanding participation in deferred retirement option plans (DROP) [3-5]. He also introduced LB76 to increase surviving spouse benefits and cost-of-living adjustments for patrol members [6, 7].
  • Community Support: LB1092 was introduced to authorize "Back the Blue" license plates, with proceeds supporting injured or fallen officers and their families [8, 9].
  • Personal Security: He introduced LB986, which allows candidates and officeholders to use campaign funds for security services and systems to protect against bona fide threats [10-12].

National Security and Foreign Influence

Bostar has been active in legislation aimed at mitigating risks from foreign adversaries:

  • Financial and Agricultural Security: He introduced LB1063 to prohibit money transmission activities by companies connected to foreign adversaries [13, 14]. He also proposed the Preventing Lethal Agricultural and National Threats Act (LB1096), which restricts foreign adversary access to critical infrastructure and sensitive technologies while establishing penalties for the unauthorized importation of high-risk pathogens [15-17].
  • Regional Strategy: He co-introduced LB193, which redefined the membership of the Committee on Pacific Conflict to prepare for potential regional or global conflicts in the Pacific theater [18, 19].

Artificial Intelligence and Technology Regulation

Bostar has emerged as a key regulator of emerging technologies:

  • AI Safety: He introduced the Conversational Artificial Intelligence Safety Act (LB1185), requiring disclosures when users interact with AI and setting guardrails to protect minors from deceptive or manipulative AI interactions [20, 21].
  • Consumer Protection: His Artificial Intelligence Consumer Protection Act (LB642) aims to safeguard consumers from algorithmic discrimination [22, 23].
  • Criminal Misuse: Through LB1020, he prioritized addressing modern technological crimes, including stalking via mobile tracking devices, the misuse of unmanned aircraft systems in restricted areas, and the creation of the offense of "swatting" [24-26].

Healthcare and Human Services

Healthcare reform, particularly for vulnerable populations and Medicaid recipients, is a core priority:

  • Medicaid and Specialized Care: He introduced LB1091 to ensure that long-term care clients with complex medical needs receive services through fee-for-service reimbursement rather than standard managed care practices [27, 28].
  • Coverage Expansion: Bostar prioritized insurance and Medicaid coverage for biomarker testing (LB253) and non-opioid drugs (LB252) to improve treatment precision and reduce opioid addiction risks [29-32]. He also co-sponsored coverage for HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (LB715) [33, 34].
  • System Modernization: LB950 seeks to expand health information technology, authorizing electronic submission for prior authorization requests and increasing participation in the state’s health information exchange [35-37].

Energy and Economic Development

  • Energy Regulation: He introduced the Large Load Customer Regulation Act (LB1064), requiring public power suppliers to establish standards for interconnecting customers with massive electricity demands (over 20MW) to maintain system reliability and minimize rate impacts [38, 39]. He also co-introduced LB1193 to tax and regulate energy storage resources [40, 41].
  • Tax Incentives: Bostar introduced LB710 to increase the state’s earned income tax credit from 10% to 20% to support working families [42, 43]. He also proposed LB1131, which would eliminate tax exemptions for data centers to fund a new tax credit for domestic violence and human trafficking service providers [44, 45].
  • Juvenile Justice: He prioritized the Juvenile Probation Administration Act (LB684) to transfer juvenile probation functions to a new executive branch agency [46, 47].
