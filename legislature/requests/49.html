What were the impacts on the Department of Transportation this session?

2026-08-24 23:44:42 · Sessions 109B · standard

Nebraska Legislature 109th Legislature, 2nd Session

The Department of Transportation (NDOT) experienced several significant regulatory, programmatic, and budgetary impacts during this legislative session. These include the establishment of new infrastructure and rail programs, work zone safety mandates, updated funding rules, and specific biennial appropriations and fund transfers.

New Regulatory & Programmatic Responsibilities

  • Infrastructure Development Investment Program Act (IDIP): NDOT is designated as the administrator and operational support provider for this newly created state infrastructure bank [1-5]. Administered by the State Highway Commission, the program provides loans and financial assistance to political subdivisions and eligible entities for qualified transportation projects [1, 3-6]. NDOT is authorized to receive and evaluate unsolicited public-private partnership (P3) proposals, charge a review fee up to \$500, and must submit an annual report to the Legislature regarding considered or approved partnerships [5, 7-10].
  • State Rail Plan Act: NDOT is designated as the authority responsible for preparing, maintaining, and administering a comprehensive, federally compliant state rail plan that addresses passenger and freight needs [11, 12]. This includes conducting an annual study of passenger rail service and reporting findings to the Legislature [12, 13].
  • Speed Control Enforcement in Work Zones: Under new legislation, NDOT is given duties to support the enforcement of speed limits in highway work zones using speed control enforcement systems and automatic license plate readers [14-16].
  • Highway District Revenue Allocation: NDOT must account for highway revenue raised by each highway district, ensuring that at least 70% of the revenue raised in a district is allocated to projects within that same district [17, 18]. System preservation remains the department's primary priority [17, 18].
  • Bidder Prequalification Exemptions: NDOT is authorized to exempt bidders from prequalification requirements for routine, specialized maintenance contracts, such as turf maintenance, ditch cleaning, culvert repair, rest area/weigh station maintenance, and intelligent transportation systems [5, 19].

Financial Allocations & Operating Appropriations

NDOT received direct appropriations from the Highway Cash Fund to the Roads Operations Cash Fund in the amount of \$543,900,000 for FY2025-26 and \$550,700,000 for FY2026-27 [20, 21]. Total department expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems are capped at \$155,441,627 for FY2025-26 and \$161,284,354 for FY2026-27 [20, 21].

Specific program-level estimated cash fund appropriations for the biennium include:

  • Construction (Program 569): \$1,038,071,186 for FY2025-26 and \$1,043,564,205 for FY2026-27 [22-24].
  • Maintenance (Program 574): \$191,865,235 for FY2025-26 and \$196,539,047 for FY2026-27 [23-25].
  • Nebraska Broadband Office (Program 562): Cash Fund estimates of \$1,720,839 (FY25-26) and \$1,721,675 (FY26-27), with estimated Federal Funds of \$139,960,000 and \$93,640,000 respectively for state aid [22-24].
  • Public Airports (Program 301): Estimated Cash Funds of \$59,348,300 for FY2025-26 and \$51,348,300 for FY2026-27 to be used as state aid [26-28].
  • Service and Support (Program 572): \$35,771,658 for FY2025-26 and \$36,213,768 for FY2026-27 [25, 29, 30].
  • Highway Administration (Program 568): \$24,560,776 for FY2025-26 and \$25,451,874 for FY2026-27 [22-24].
  • Local Transit Authorities Assistance (Program 305): \$6,312,705 each fiscal year, including specific state aid carve-outs for intercity bus assistance (\$535,000) and public transportation assistance (\$5,777,705) [26-28].
  • Facility Improvements (Program 901): NDOT is authorized to prepare program statements on, design, and build Falls City maintenance and general maintenance projects, with \$4,800,000 in future Cash Funds appropriated for FY2026-27 [25, 29, 30]. Unobligated capital construction balances will lapse upon project completion or postponement [31-33].

Required Fund Transfers & Inter-Agency Payments

  • Law Enforcement in Construction Zones: NDOT is required to contract with the Nebraska State Patrol for law enforcement coverage along state road construction zones, transferring \$489,979 each fiscal year from the Roads Operations Cash Fund to the Nebraska State Patrol Cash Fund in four equal quarterly payments [34-39].
  • Carrier Enforcement Cash Fund: Transfers from the Roads Operations Cash Fund to the Carrier Enforcement Cash Fund are set at \$13,075,900 for FY2025-26 (less any unexpended balance existing on June 30, 2025) [40-42]. For FY2026-27, the transfer is set at \$13,325,900 [43-45] (amended to \$13,710,157 in some subsequent bills) [8, 46].
  • County Bridge Match Program: The State Treasurer is directed to transfer \$4,000,000 (reduced from \$5,000,000) from the Roads Operations Cash Fund to the Transportation Infrastructure Bank Fund on or before June 30, 2026 and June 30, 2027 to fund the program [109B-47].
  • Tort Claims Transfers: Specific transfers are mandated from NDOT's agency fund balances to the Tort Claims Fund, including \$30,934 (due by August 1, 2025) and \$29,267 (due by August 1, 2026) [48-50].
  • Transportation Services Bureau Fund: The State Treasurer is directed to transfer \$12,285 from the administratively created Transportation Services Bureau Revolving Fund to the General Fund before June 30, 2026 [51-55].
