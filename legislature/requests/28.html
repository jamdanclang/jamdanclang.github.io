How many income tax bills were passed in the last 5 years?

2026-08-01 19:55:07 · Sessions 109B · deep

To: Legislative Research Review Committee From: Senior Legislative Research Analyst Date: August 1, 2026 Subject: Review of Income Tax Legislation Passed (2022–2026)

Based on a review of the records from the One Hundred Ninth Legislature and relevant previous session references, at least ten primary bills directly relating to Nebraska income tax—including deductions, credits, and substantive adjustments to the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967—were confirmed as passed or referenced as having passed within the last five years.

Confirmed Primary Income Tax Legislation (2026 Session)

The One Hundred Ninth Legislature, Second Session (2026), saw the passage and gubernatorial approval of seven bills that significantly impact state income tax:

1. LB 748 (Approved April 14, 2026): Amends provisions of the Nebraska educational savings plan trust, allowing trust funds to be used for recognized postsecondary credential programs and adjusting related state income tax adjustments [1, 2]. 2. LB 803 (Passed with Emergency Clause, April 2026): Adopts the First-Time Home Buyer Savings Account Act, which provides an income tax deduction (reduction to federal adjusted gross income) for contributions to eligible savings accounts [3-5]. 3. LB 901 (Approved April 7, 2026): Adopts the Domestic Violence and Human Trafficking Service Providers Tax Credit Act, creating refundable and transferable income tax credits [6, 7]. 4. LB 954 (Approved April 14, 2026): Amends the Nebraska Advantage Act to require the Department of Revenue to recalculate base-year employees for Tier 6 projects in specific sale or transfer scenarios, affecting eligibility for income tax incentives [8, 9]. 5. LB 998 (Approved April 14, 2026): Modifies the state income tax deduction for members of the Nebraska National Guard to ensure equitable tax treatment across various military duty statuses [10, 11]. 6. LB 1048 (Approved April 14, 2026): Amends section 77-27,238 to provide income tax credits for employers who provide tuition assistance or transportation to eligible employees [12, 13]. 7. LB 1165 (Approved April 16, 2026): Adopts the Grow the Good Life Act, which provides a new income tax credit for certain large employers undergoing ownership changes and modifies credit percentages under the ImagiNE Nebraska Act [14, 15].

*Note: Major appropriations bills LB 1071 [16] and LB 1209 [17], both approved in April 2026, also included substantive amendments to section 77-2717, which governs the income tax imposed on estates and trusts.*

Confirmed/Referenced Legislation (2022–2025)

The sources reference three additional pieces of legislation passed during the preceding years of the five-year window:

  • LB 261 (2025): This 2025 appropriations act included amendments to section 77-2717 of the Nebraska Revenue Act of 1967, affecting income tax for resident and nonresident estates and trusts [18, 19].
  • LB 1088 (2024): Confirmed as passed in 2024, this bill modified sections 77-5723 and 77-5727 of the Nebraska Advantage Act, impacting active tax incentive agreements [20, 21].
  • LB 1150 (2022): Confirmed as passed in 2022, this legislation amended several sections of the Nebraska Advantage Act (77-5705, 77-5723, 77-5725, 77-5726, 77-5727, and 77-5731), directly affecting income tax benefit calculations and elections [20, 21].

Areas of Uncertainty

While the 2025 and 2026 sessions included the introduction of numerous other tax-related bills—such as LB 107 (renter's credit), LB 171 (tax rate changes), and LB 710 (earned income tax credit increase)—these primarily exist in the record as "Introduced" versions [22-24]. Some, like LB 1109 (eliminating renewable energy credits), were incorporated into other successful bills (specifically LB 901) during the amendment process [25, 26]. However, for those introduced bills not explicitly listed as "Approved by the Governor" or "Passed," their final status as law cannot be confirmed from the provided documentation.
